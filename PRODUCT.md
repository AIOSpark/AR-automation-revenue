# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Enterprise CFOs, AR Managers, and Finance Teams managing complex B2B receivables, invoice dispute resolution, and AI-driven collections workflows.

## Product Purpose
Autonomous B2B Accounts Receivable Automation & Revenue Intelligence Workstation (AIOSpark). Converts locked cash into working capital via autonomous agents, root-cause exception resolution, cash unblock simulation, and human-in-the-loop approvals.

## Positioning
An intelligent, human-controlled AR automation workstation that replaces generic "AI dark-mode templates" with a warm, editorial, tactile financial command center built for high-density clarity and audit-grade trust.

## Operating Context
Daily AR operations, invoice tracking, automated collections, cash-unblock forecasting, CFO financial reporting, customer risk scoring, and agent policy verification.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Real-time receivables tracking and aging metrics (0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days)
- 7-step AI Agent loop (Observe, Collect, Diagnose, Plan, Execute, Verify, Report)
- Interactive Cash Unblock simulator & Causal Graph root-cause tree
- Human control guardrails, approval queues, and RBAC
- Enterprise ERP / Tally / Zoho / Bank integration sync

## Brand Commitments
- **Design Pattern**: Warm Editorial & Tactile Financial Workstation
- **Backgrounds**: Deep warm charcoal/obsidian (`#0B0C0E`), warm elevated stone cards (`#14161B`, `#1C1F27`)
- **Accents**: Warm Crimson signal (`#FF3B30`), Amber/Gold warnings (`#F59E0B`), Muted Sage accents (`#10B981`)
- **Typography**: Editorial serif display accents for headings, ultra-clean sans-serif for UI, tabular mono for monetary figures (`font-mono-num`)
- **Textures & Micro-details**: Subtly embossed borders, warm paper/leather touch cues, warm subtle glow highlights, tactile buttons with spring feel

## Evidence on Hand
- Complete Next.js 16 codebase with app router (`src/app/`)
- Existing financial data models, adapters, and UI component hierarchy

## Product Principles
1. **Human-Centered Craft**: Rejects bland AI clichés (neon blue glows, floating cards, abstract particles). Features warm, tactile financial surfaces designed like a luxury instrument.
2. **Financial Telemetry & Precision**: High-density numeric data display using tabular fonts, crisp column rules, and fast visual scanning.
3. **Transparent Autonomy**: AI agents present clear causal steps, verification proof, and human override controls at every decision point.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High visual contrast (WCAG AA compliant) for dense financial tables
- Distinct color + icon indicators for status (never rely on color alone)
- Keyboard focus rings and ARIA labels on interactive elements
